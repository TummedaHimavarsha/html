Jalsa is a Telugu action-comedy-drama that blends humor, romance, and emotion around the life of Sanjay Sahu, a spirited and carefree youth with a rebellious past. Once a student revolutionary, Sanjay now leads a modest life as a software trainer. His charm and honesty draw the attention of two sisters — Jo and Bhagyamathi — leading to a fun and emotional love triangle.

🎬 Record-Breaking Audio Launch: The movie’s audio launch was one of the grandest events in Tollywood at the time — it was released by Mahesh Babu, who rarely appeared at other movie events! 🎵 Massive Musical Hit: The soundtrack composed by Devi Sri Prasad (DSP) was a blockbuster. Every song — especially “My Heart is Beating” and “Gaallo Thelinattunde” — became chart-toppers across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.
